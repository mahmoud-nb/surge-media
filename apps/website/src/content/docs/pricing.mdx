<Section id="faq" eyebrow="FAQ" title="Frequently asked questions">
<div class="sm-prose">

### Which plan applies to us?

The one that matches the gross revenue of your organization **aggregated with its affiliates** (entities controlling, controlled by, or under common control with it), for its **last completed fiscal year**, excluding VAT (the same criterion as the license). Other currencies are converted at the Federal Reserve rate of the last business day of that year. Under USD 500,000, production use is free.

### What is a production instance?

One SurgeMedia database used in production, with all the web servers and video workers that share it. Development, testing and staging instances are free and unlimited in every plan. Need more production instances than your plan includes? Tell us and we will add them to your license.

### How do we order and pay?

Order by email: we reply with an invoice and, once it is paid, a license file that you install in **Settings › License** or with `NUXT_LICENSE_KEY`. Payment is by bank transfer; purchase orders are accepted on the Business plan and above. Paying two years in advance gets 10% off, three years 15%.

### Is SurgeMedia open source?

Not in the OSI sense, at least not today. SurgeMedia is **source-available** under the Business Source License 1.1: you can read, modify and self-host the code, and use it in production for free unless an exclusion applies (revenue above USD 500k, a competing hosted service, or a licensed feature used without a key, though no such feature exists today). Each version converts to the **Apache License 2.0** three years after its release.

### Do the front-end components need a license?

No. `@surge-media/client` and `@surge-media/vue` are MIT-licensed: free for everyone, in any project. The Business Source License conditions only concern the SurgeMedia server (`@surge-media/core`) you install.

### We are an agency. Can we install SurgeMedia for our clients?

Yes. Running a dedicated instance for a client, solely for that client's websites and apps, as part of your design, development or maintenance services, is **not** a competing hosted service. The revenue threshold then applies to both you and your client. The Agency Partner program covers your own instances and gives you a margin on your clients' licenses: [contact us](mailto:licensing@surgeup.org?subject=SurgeMedia%20Agency%20Partner%20license) for the terms that fit your case.

### What counts as a “competing hosted service”?

Offering SurgeMedia, modified or not, to third parties as a hosted, managed or “as a service” product whose main purpose is to let them store, manage, process or deliver **their own media**: a media storage service, a headless media CMS, etc. Using SurgeMedia for your own products, even when your users upload media through them, is not one. It requires a specific agreement.

### Do you meter images, bandwidth or transformations?

No. SurgeMedia runs on your infrastructure: storage, bandwidth and CPU are yours, and so are the savings. Licenses depend on the size of your organization and your number of production instances, never on volume.

### Can we try everything before buying?

Yes. Development, testing and evaluation are free for everyone, with every feature: no trial period, no license key. The [live demo](https://demo.usesurgemedia.com) needs no installation at all.

### What about architecture, security or operations?

The [full FAQ](../faq/) answers questions from CIOs, security officers, engineering teams and buyers, with the actual status of every feature.
