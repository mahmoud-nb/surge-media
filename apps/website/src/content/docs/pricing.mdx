<div class="sm-prose">

### Is surgeMedia open source?

Not in the OSI sense, today. surgeMedia is **source-available** under the Business Source License 1.1: you can read, modify and self-host the code, and use it in production for free unless one of the two exclusions applies (revenue above USD 500k, or a competing hosted service). Each version converts to the **Apache License 2.0** three years after its release.

### Do the front-end components need a license?

No. `@surge-media/client` and `@surge-media/vue` are MIT-licensed: free for everyone, in any project. The Business Source License conditions only concern the surgeMedia server (`@surge-media/core`) you install.

### How is the USD 500,000 threshold calculated?

It is the gross revenue of the organization using surgeMedia — or on whose behalf it is used — **aggregated with its affiliates** (entities controlling, controlled by, or under common control with it), for its **last completed fiscal year**, excluding VAT. Other currencies are converted at the Federal Reserve rate of the last business day of that year.

### We are an agency. Can we install surgeMedia for our clients?

Yes. Running a dedicated instance for a client, solely for that client's websites and apps, as part of your design, development or maintenance services, is **not** a competing hosted service. The revenue threshold then applies to both you and your client. The Pro / Agency license covers agencies or clients above it.

### What counts as a “competing hosted service”?

Offering surgeMedia — modified or not — to third parties as a hosted, managed or “as a service” product whose main purpose is to let them store, manage, process or deliver **their own media**: a media storage service, a headless media CMS, etc. Using surgeMedia for your own products, even when your users upload media through them, is not one.

### Do you meter images, bandwidth or transformations?

No. surgeMedia runs on your infrastructure: storage, bandwidth and CPU are yours, and so are the savings. Licenses are not tied to volume.

### Can we try everything before buying?

Yes. Development, testing and evaluation are free for everyone, with every feature — no trial period, no license key.
