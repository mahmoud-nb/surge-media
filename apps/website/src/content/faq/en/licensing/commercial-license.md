Only in production, and only in three cases:

- your organization, together with its group companies, made more than **USD 500,000** in revenue in its last completed fiscal year;
- you offer surgeMedia to third parties as a hosted media service;
- you use in production a feature that requires a license key (none today: they will be listed on the [License](../license/) page).

Development, testing and evaluation are always free.
