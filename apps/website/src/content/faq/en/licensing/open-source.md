Its source code is **public**, under the Business Source License 1.1: you can read it, modify it and self-host it. It is not an OSI open source license yet, but each version automatically moves to **Apache 2.0** three years after its release.

The front-end libraries `@surge-media/client` and `@surge-media/vue` are MIT-licensed.
