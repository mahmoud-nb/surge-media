Community support on GitHub for the free tier, priority support for the Pro / Agency plan, and dedicated support with a service level agreement (SLA) for the Enterprise plan, which also includes onboarding and an architecture review.

Whatever the plan, start with `surge-media diagnose`: it prints a report without secrets or personal data (version, configuration, database, storage, findings) to attach to your request.
