Per organization and per year, according to its size (gross revenue of the group): Pro USD 1,200 (USD 0.5M to 5M), Business USD 4,800 (USD 5M to 50M), Enterprise from USD 18,000, Enterprise Global from USD 40,000. There is no usage metering at all: no image, transformation or bandwidth counts.

Every plan includes every media feature; the plans differ by their number of production instances, their support and the enterprise features (single sign-on, SIEM export). Development, testing and staging instances are free and unlimited, and organizations that sign before version 1.0 keep their price for three years.
