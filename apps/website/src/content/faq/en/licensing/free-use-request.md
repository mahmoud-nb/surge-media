No. Free production use depends only on the conditions of the license (revenue, competing hosted service, licensed features), never on a mention or a declaration. We do ask for one, though: tell us who you are, with the optional **Register this instance** button in **Settings › License** or by e-mail at licensing@surgeup.org, or mention SurgeMedia where you credit the tools you use.

In return, you are notified of security advisories and major releases, and your feedback weighs on the roadmap. Your organization is listed among SurgeMedia users only with your written agreement.
