---
question: "What happens if the vendor goes out of business?"
order: 6
keywords: ["continuity","reversibility","lock-in","vendor","supplier risk","escrow"]
---
Your instance keeps running: it depends on no service of the vendor.

You keep the source code and the right to maintain it, your data stays in your database and storage in standard formats, and every version becomes open source (Apache 2.0) three years after its release.
