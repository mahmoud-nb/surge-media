Yes: report issues and suggest improvements on GitHub, where pull requests are welcome. Before a first pull request is merged, its author accepts the Contributor License Agreement: you keep the copyright of your work and grant the publisher the right to distribute it, including under commercial licenses. The [contributing guide](https://github.com/mahmoud-nb/surge-media/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) explains how. For a security issue, follow the [security policy](https://github.com/mahmoud-nb/surge-media/blob/main/SECURITY.md) instead.
