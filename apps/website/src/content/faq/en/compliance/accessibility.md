The administration and the front-end components target **WCAG 2.2 AA**: keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen reader announcements, and contrast checked in both light and dark themes. Every page is audited with axe-core.

For your own websites, SurgeMedia stores alternative text, captions and audio descriptions, and its components render them.
