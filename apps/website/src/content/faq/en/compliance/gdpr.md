---
question: "How does surgeMedia fit into our GDPR compliance?"
order: 1
keywords: ["gdpr","rgpd","personal data","controller","processor","dpa"]
---
You remain the sole data controller: surgeMedia runs on your premises and the vendor has no access to any data, so it is not your processor.

The personal data processed is limited to accounts (email, name), sessions (IP address, browser), the audit log and the content of the media you upload. Passwords and tokens are never stored in plain text.
