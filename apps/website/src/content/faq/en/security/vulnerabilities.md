Through GitHub's private reporting form, never in a public issue: the [security policy](https://github.com/mahmoud-nb/surge-media/blob/main/SECURITY.md) explains how.

Our targets: an acknowledgement within 3 business days, a fix within 14 days for a critical issue and 30 days for a high one, then a public security advisory. Dependencies are checked weekly with Dependabot and audited before every release.
