---
question: "Is SurgeMedia a DAM?"
order: 4
status: partial
keywords: ["dam","digital asset management","workflow","approval","usage rights"]
---
Partly. SurgeMedia covers media organization (projects, folders, tags, search, versions, trash), access rights and optimized delivery.

It has no approval workflow, no usage-rights management (credits, licenses, expiry dates) and no custom metadata fields. It works well alongside a DAM that stays the editorial source, as its delivery layer.
