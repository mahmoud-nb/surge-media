---
question: "Who is surgeMedia for?"
order: 2
keywords: ["audience","use cases","agency","sovereignty","who"]
---
Teams that want to keep their media and their costs under control:

- publishers of websites and apps that serve a lot of images and videos;
- agencies that run one instance per client;
- organizations with sovereignty, security or compliance requirements;
- teams that want one media library for development, staging and production.
