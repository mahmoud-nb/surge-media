surgeMedia is a **self-hosted** media platform: it stores, transforms and delivers your images and videos from your own servers, under your own domain.

Your teams manage media in an administration interface; your websites and apps consume it through a **REST API** and CDN-ready **delivery URLs**, in the right format and at the right size. It is a *headless* media back end: no CMS, front end or hosting provider is imposed.
