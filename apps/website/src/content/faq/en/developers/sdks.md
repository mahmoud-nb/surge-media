- `@surge-media/client`: a dependency-free JavaScript client (API, image URLs, upload queue);
- `@surge-media/vue`: display and upload components (`SmImage`, `SmPicture`, `SmVideo`, `SmUploader`…).

Both are MIT-licensed. React, Svelte and Nuxt packages are planned. For another language, generate a client from the OpenAPI document.
