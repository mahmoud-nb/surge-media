question: "Which file formats are accepted?"

- **Images**: JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF and GIF, up to 100 megapixels.
- **Videos**: MP4, MOV and WebM; with FFmpeg enabled, also MKV, AVI, 3GP, MPEG-TS, OGV and WMV.
- **Subtitles**: WebVTT and SRT.

The type is detected from the file's contents, never from its name. SVG, HEIC and TIFF are rejected; audio and documents are planned.
