question: "Which image transformations are available?"
order: 4
status: available
keywords: ["transformation","resize","crop","quality","format","preset"]
related:
  - label: "Image transformations"
    href: "/reference/image-transformations/"
---
Resizing, five fit modes, cropping (exact area, focal point or smart crop), rotation, mirroring, quality, background color, pixel density, and output as JPEG, PNG, WebP or AVIF. An image is never upscaled.

Each transformation is computed once, then served as a file. A per-project policy can restrict generation to signed URLs or to named presets.
